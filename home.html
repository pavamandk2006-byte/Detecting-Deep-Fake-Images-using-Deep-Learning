About DeepGuard

DeepGuard was developed as a final-year project to demonstrate the application of artificial intelligence and computer vision in deepfake image detection. The system uses an EfficientNet-B0 model trained on real and deepfake facial images. An OpenCV face detector is used to locate faces before classification.
